02 · What we teach

The elementary math skills that create downstream leverage.

The goal is not to complete tonight's worksheet. It is to strengthen the mathematics your child will keep using in middle school and beyond.

Core foundations

Build fluency without sacrificing understanding.

  • Number sense and place value
  • Multiplication and division fluency
  • Fractions and fraction operations
  • Decimals and proportional thinking

Reasoning and readiness

Raise the ceiling for capable students.

  • Explaining and justifying mathematical thinking
  • Flexible problem-solving strategies
  • Multi-step word problems
  • Pre-algebra and middle-school readiness

Common questions

What parents usually want to know about private math tutoring.

What grades do you tutor?

Algebra Ready specializes in Grades 4–6. That is the window where fluency, fraction understanding, decimals, number sense, and reasoning become especially important for later algebra success.

Is this homework help?

Homework can show me where a student is struggling, but homework completion is not the product. Sessions target the underlying skill or misconception that will matter beyond one assignment.

What if my child is already doing well?

That can still be a strong fit. Some students need extension rather than remediation. The assessment looks at depth, flexibility, and readiness—not only whether a child is passing class.

How do we start?

Start with a free parent call, a free student math assessment, and a personalized written Math Game Plan. Keep the plan with no obligation. If you want help putting it into practice, hire Matthew for paid, one-on-one tutoring in your home.
