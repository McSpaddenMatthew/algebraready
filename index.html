06 · Readiness & fit

The right structure for the right student.

Selectivity protects the quality of the group and the attention each student receives.

A strong fit

Capable. Curious. Ready for more.

  • Earns acceptable grades but is not consistently challenged
  • Knows procedures but struggles to explain why they work
  • Has gaps in fractions, number sense, or multi-step reasoning
  • Is preparing for advanced middle-school mathematics

Not the right fit

Clear boundaries protect results.

  • You are looking primarily for homework completion or drop-in tutoring
  • Your child needs intensive intervention beyond a small-group setting
  • Weekly attendance and short between-session practice are not realistic

08 · Common questions

Clear answers before the first call.

Is this only for students who are struggling?

No. Algebra Ready is built for capable students who need stronger foundations, greater challenge, or both. Good grades can coexist with unfinished reasoning or untapped potential.

How can semi-private instruction still be personal?

Students are matched around a shared mathematical domain, but each enters at the point shown by the assessment. The group creates discussion and energy; the roadmap, prompts, and next steps remain individual.

Do we have to enroll after the Readiness Review?

No. The parent call, student assessment, and results meeting come first. You see the evidence and receive a clear recommendation before making any enrollment decision.

What happens after the initial 12 weeks?

We compare the baseline with the final reassessment. Your child either graduates with a written next-step plan or receives a focused recommendation to continue in four-week Academy evidence cycles.
