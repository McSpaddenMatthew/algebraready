05 · Readiness & fit

Made for the right student—not every student.

Small groups work only when students are a good match. That is why the assessment comes before enrollment.

Likely a strong fit

A capable student who needs a stronger skill or more challenge.

  • Earns solid grades but is not often challenged
  • Can follow steps but struggles to explain why they work
  • Has a gap in fractions, number sense, or multi-step problems
  • Needs a stronger base before middle-school math

Better served another way

Not every student needs this group.

  • You are looking primarily for homework completion or drop-in tutoring
  • Your child needs more intensive one-to-one support
  • Weekly attendance and short between-session practice are not realistic

07 · Common questions

Clear answers before the first call.

Is this only for students who are struggling?

No. Algebra Ready is built for capable students who need stronger foundations, greater challenge, or both. Good grades can sit beside a weak math skill or work that is too easy.

What exactly does Academy enrollment include?

The initial commitment is twelve weeks: one 60-minute session each week in a group of four or fewer, a personal plan, three short practice sets each week, weekly parent updates, progress checks, a final assessment, and a written next step.

What is the first-session win?

The first lesson begins with a problem your child missed on the assessment. After focused teaching, we check a closely matched problem. Your first parent note shows what your child did differently and what comes next.

How can semi-private instruction still be personal?

Students are matched around a shared math skill, but each starts where the assessment places them. The group adds discussion and energy. The plan, questions, and next steps stay personal.

Who leads my child’s academic plan?

Matthew reviews the assessments, designs each student’s plan, and checks progress. Matthew or a carefully selected Algebra Ready teacher follows that plan in the weekly sessions.

Where and when do groups meet?

Groups meet in person in the Charleston area. Because students are matched by readiness and grade, current location, day, and time options are shared during the parent call.

Do we have to enroll after the Readiness Review?

No. The parent call, student assessment, and results meeting come first. You see the evidence and receive a clear recommendation before making any enrollment decision.

What happens after the initial 12 weeks?

We compare the final check with where your child started. Your child either finishes with a written next step or receives a clear recommendation to continue for another four weeks.
