01 · The academy

Built for students with more range.

Strong grades are welcome. They are not the final standard. Algebra Ready serves capable students whose present performance may not yet reveal the depth, fluency, and reasoning required by harder mathematics.

01

Narrow by design

A focused grade band allows instruction to anticipate the precise transition into advanced middle-school mathematics.

02

Small-group rigor

Students learn to articulate, challenge, and refine mathematical thinking—not simply complete more problems.

03

Readiness-based entry

Every family begins with a substantive review. We enroll, defer, or redirect according to genuine academic fit.

04

Visible evidence

Baseline measures, work samples, reassessment, and a clear roadmap replace vague promises of progress.

03 · The readiness sprint

Eight weeks.
One coherent academic experience.

The Sprint is not eight unrelated tutoring sessions. It is a measured progression from diagnosis to independent performance, with parents given visibility throughout.
